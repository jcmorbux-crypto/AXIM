FONT
The exact AXIM wordmark is custom artwork and should be used as an image/vector asset, not recreated by typing a font.
Closest practical UI/body font: Inter.
Closest practical wordmark starting points for future vector redrawing: Montserrat ExtraBold, Gotham Bold, or Avenir Next Heavy, followed by custom modification of the X.

LOCKED X RULE
The upper-right segment stops before the center crossing. Do not reconnect it or replace it with a conventional X.
